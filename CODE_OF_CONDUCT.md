# Code of conduct

Glint follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, the wiki and any other project space.

## In short

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent, and criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and questions. Everyone was new once.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, or publishing other people's private information.
- Accept that maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant linked above is the authoritative version, including its enforcement guidelines.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer, [@smith8ca](https://github.com/smith8ca), using the contact options on their GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's own **Report content** option on any comment, issue or pull request.

Reports are handled confidentially. Please don't raise conduct problems in public issues.
